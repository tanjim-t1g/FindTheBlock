Survival Milestone Buffs

Why FindTheBlock grants Speed II and Regeneration I at the 20-minute mark.

In competitive scavenger matches, rounds that exceed 15 to 20 minutes can occasionally turn into stalemates where players struggle to find rare underground structures or materials. FindTheBlock introduces an automated milestone buff system to keep late-game matches fast and dynamic.

Why We Replaced Elytras

In early testing, granting an Elytra at 20 minutes caused three severe issues on production servers:

  • Server Chunk Overload: When multiple players fly with Elytras simultaneously, the server must generate and load dozens of new chunks every second. This quickly exhausts CPU and disk resources, dropping TPS below 15.
  • Firework Dependency: An Elytra is useless without fireworks. Players who had not crafted rockets could not fly, while players with rockets gained an unfair advantage.
  • Skips Survival Gameplay: Flying 200 blocks in the air lets players skip surface biomes, ravines, and caves rather than exploring the terrain.

The Speed II & Regeneration I Solution

When a match reaches the 20-minute mark (1,200 seconds), all active racers automatically receive permanent Speed II and Regeneration I status effects:

  • Speed II: Increases walking and sprinting velocity by 40%. Players traverse mountains, valleys, and cave systems quickly on foot without causing chunk loading lag.
  • Regeneration I: Continuously restores health (1 heart every 2.5 seconds), reducing downtime from fall damage, lava, and hostile mob attacks during intense late-game searches.

Configuring the Buff Timer

You can adjust the milestone threshold or modify the potion effects in config.yml:

config.yml 
buffs:
  enabled: true
  trigger-time-seconds: 1200   # Elapsed match time in seconds (1200 = 20 mins)
  effects:
    - type: SPEED
      amplifier: 1              # Speed II (amplifier is 0-indexed)
    - type: REGENERATION
      amplifier: 0              # Regeneration I
