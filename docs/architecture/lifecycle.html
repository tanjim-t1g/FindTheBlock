Match Lifecycle

The finite state machine that governs player flow from lobby countdown to podium victory.

FindTheBlock manages rounds through five distinct states enforced by GameManager.java:

[LOBBY] ---> (/ftb start) ---> [COUNTDOWN] ---> (Timer Expires) ---> [ACTIVE]
                                                                        |
                                                                (Target Acquired)
                                                                        |
                                                                        v
[LOBBY] <--- (Cleanup Done) <--- [RESETTING] <--- (Celebration) <--- [PODIUM]

1. LOBBY State

Players wait on the bedrock platform in the lobby world. During this state:

  • PVP and player damage are cancelled.
  • Hunger loss is disabled (food level stays at 20).
  • Block placing and breaking are blocked for all non-operators.
  • When an operator runs /ftb start, the plugin checks if enough players are online (configured by minimum-players).

2. COUNTDOWN State

When a match is initiated:

  • A 10-second auditory countdown begins with sound pings.
  • The interactive RouletteMenu opens for all participants, cycling through candidate items before locking onto the final target block.
  • The background world factory completes pre-loading chunks in the fresh Overworld and Nether worlds.

3. ACTIVE State

When the countdown reaches zero:

  • Players are scattered across safe coordinates in the match Overworld.
  • A persistent BossBar is displayed showing the target item name and remaining time.
  • AcquisitionListener listens for item acquisitions:
    • Picking up an item from the ground.
    • Crafting an item in a crafting table.
    • Smelting an item in a furnace or blast furnace.
    • Brewing a potion or taking an item out of a chest.

4. PODIUM State

The instant any player acquires the target item:

  • The round immediately stops and advances to PODIUM.
  • All players are teleported back to the lobby platform where a 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place podium is constructed.
  • Automated firework volleys launch, a victory title appears on screen, and the winner's time is broadcast to chat.
  • The win is recorded permanently in stats.yml for leaderboards.

5. RESETTING State

After a 15-second celebration period:

  • Player inventories are cleared and potion effects removed.
  • The temporary match Overworld and Nether worlds are unloaded and deleted.
  • The state returns to LOBBY, ready for the next round.
