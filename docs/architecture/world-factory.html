World Factory & Nether

How FindTheBlock generates fresh match worlds and links Nether dimensions without dropping server TPS.

Generating new Minecraft worlds on the main server thread causes noticeable lag spikes and freezes. FindTheBlock solves this with a background world generation engine handled by WorldFactory.java and ChunkPacer.java.

Overworld & Nether World Pairing

Every match requires access to both Overworld materials (like wood, stone, iron) and Nether materials (like quartz, blaze powder, nether wart). For every round, the plugin creates a linked pair of worlds:

  • ftb_<id>: The primary match Overworld dimension.
  • ftb_<id>_nether: The linked Nether dimension, generated using the exact same random world seed.

How Nether Portals Work

Vanilla Minecraft relies on fixed dimension names (like world and world_nether). When players are in a custom match world, vanilla portal logic would normally get confused. FindTheBlock's PortalListener.java intercepts portal teleportation and calculates coordinates dynamically:

  • Overworld to Nether: When a player enters a nether portal in the match Overworld, they are teleported to the match Nether at X / 8, Y, Z / 8.
  • Nether to Overworld: When a player steps into a portal in the match Nether, they are returned to the match Overworld at X * 8, Y, Z * 8.
What About The End?
FindTheBlock intentionally does not generate an End dimension. Finding a Stronghold, gathering 12 Eyes of Ender, and defeating the Ender Dragon takes much longer than a standard rush round. Keeping the match focused on the Overworld and Nether ensures fast, competitive rounds.

Chunk Pacing (Zero Lag)

Instead of forcing the server to load hundreds of chunks all at once when players scatter, ChunkPacer enqueues chunks around the spawn center and pre-loads them asynchronously at a steady rate:

config.yml 
world-factory:
  chunk-radius: 12      # Radius of chunks pre-loaded around match spawn
  chunk-pace-rate: 30   # Chunks loaded per second to prevent tick stutter
  auto-cleanup: true    # Automatically delete match worlds when round ends

Automatic World Cleanup

When a match ends, both the Overworld and Nether temporary worlds are unloaded without saving (Bukkit.unloadWorld(world, false)). A background worker thread then deletes the world folders from your server directory, ensuring your disk never fills up with leftover match files.
