FindTheBlock

A fast item-rush minigame for Paper 1.21+ servers. Players race in a fresh world to find or craft a randomly chosen item.

The Game in 3 Simple Steps

  1. Lobby: Players gather in the lobby. Party leader or admin starts with /start.
  2. Roulette: A 10-second countdown plays, and an item roulette spins on screen to pick a target block.
  3. The Race: Players scatter into a fresh survival world with a linked Nether. First person to pick up or craft the target item wins!

Quick Start Guide

Install the plugin and start your first match in 3 easy steps.

1. Requirements

  • Server: Paper 1.21.1+ (or Purpur).
  • Java: Java 21 LTS.
  • Optional: PlaceholderAPI if using scoreboards.

2. Installation

  1. Drop FindTheBlock-1.0.0.jar into your server's plugins/ folder.
  2. Restart your server.
  3. Done! The plugin creates its files in plugins/FindTheBlock/ automatically.

3. Play a Match

  1. Join the server. You will spawn on the safe bedrock platform in the void lobby.
  2. Give yourself admin rights with /op <your_name>.
  3. Start a match:
    Chat 
    /start normal
  4. The 10-second countdown begins, the roulette picks an item, and you are scattered into the world.
  5. Forgot how to craft the target item? Type /recipe to see the crafting table recipe.
  6. Collect or craft the item to win!

World Gen & Nether

How fresh Overworld and Nether worlds are generated with zero server lag.

Background World Factory Pipeline

Traditional minigames freeze the server when creating a new world. FindTheBlock prevents lag using a background generation pipeline:

  • Standby Queue: It keeps a pre-generated world pair ready before the match starts.
  • Paced Chunk Gen: New chunks are generated asynchronously at 30 chunks per second, keeping server TPS at a locked 20.0.
  • Automatic Cleanup: When a match concludes, the temporary worlds are unloaded and wiped from disk completely.

Nether & The End Dimensions

  • Nether: An Overworld and Nether world pair is generated with the exact same seed. Nether portals link with 1:8 coordinate scaling.
  • The End: Supported on-demand. An End dimension (ftb_<id>_the_end) is only loaded if a player enters an End portal. If no one goes to the End, it is never loaded to save server resources.
  • Automatic Cleanup: When the match finishes, all match dimensions (Overworld, Nether, and End) are automatically unloaded and deleted from disk.

Match Flow

How a match progresses from lobby to victory.

The 4 Match States

State Duration What Happens
LOBBY Unlimited Players wait on the lobby platform. PvP, hunger, and damage are disabled.
STARTING 10 seconds Countdown plays on screen with sounds. Item roulette spins to pick the target block.
IN_GAME Until win Players scatter into fresh survival Overworld and Nether worlds. First pickup wins.
ENDING 10 seconds Winner celebration with fireworks. Players teleport back to lobby; match worlds delete.

Player Death & Respawn

  • Players keep inventory on death inside match worlds.
  • Respawn sends players back to their bed or match world spawn point.

Party Matchmaking & 20s Ready Check

  • Party Match Initiation: When a party leader starts a match with /start or via the Match Selector, an interactive ready-check menu opens for all party members.
  • 20-Second Window: Players click [ Accept & Join ] or [ Decline ]. If a player does not respond within 20 seconds, they are considered declined.
  • Automatic Start: Once all players respond (or 20s expires), racers who confirmed enter the 10-second countdown and roulette, while declining players stay safely in the lobby.

Stopping a Match

  • Admins can stop an active match anytime with /ftb stop.
  • All players return to the lobby immediately with cleared inventories, and temporary worlds are deleted.

Crash Reconnect

How player disconnects and mid-game joiners are handled.

60-Second Crash Grace Window

  • If a player crashes or disconnects during a match, their spot, inventory, and coordinates are held for 60 seconds.
  • If they reconnect within 60 seconds, they return directly to where they were in the match.
  • If they do not reconnect within 60 seconds, their saved session expires and they are treated as a Late Joiner (spawning in the lobby with options to join or spectate).

Late Joiners

When a player connects while a match is already running, they receive clickable chat options:

  • Join: Run /join to scatter into the match immediately (if enabled in config).
  • Spectate: Run /spectate to fly around as a spectator.
  • Stay in Lobby: Wait on the lobby platform until the next match starts.

20-Minute Milestone Buffs

Permanent speed and regeneration buffs to help close out long matches.

The Buffs

When a match reaches the 20-minute mark, all remaining players receive permanent potion buffs to speed up pacing until the match ends:

  • Speed II: Increased movement and sprinting speed.
  • Regeneration I: Steady passive health recovery.

Both buffs stay active until the match ends.

Void Lobby

The default pre-game waiting area included with FindTheBlock.

Platform Specs

  • World name: ftb_lobby (created automatically if missing).
  • Size: 100 × 100 bedrock platform centered around (0, 0).
  • Elevation: Y = 100.
  • Spawn point: X = 0.5, Y = 101.0, Z = 0.5.

Void Boundary Protection

  • If a player falls or jumps off the platform, they are caught at Y = 90.
  • The player is instantly teleported back to the center spawn.
  • No fall damage is dealt, and no death screen is triggered.

Lobby Features & Controls

  • Match Selector Compass: Given automatically in Slot 4. Right-click to browse active matches, join/spectate mid-game, or access the Party Finder.
  • In-World Leaderboard: Admins can spawn floating text leaderboards with /ftb leaderboard spawn [wins|time] anywhere on the platform.
  • Clean Inventories: Returning to the lobby clears survival gear so players never carry tools or ores into the lobby.

Lobby Rules

  • PvP is disabled.
  • Hunger does not drain.
  • Weather is locked to clear daylight.
  • Blocks cannot be broken or placed.

Change Lobby World

How to use your own custom spawn, hub build, or multi-world lobby instead of the auto-generated void platform.

Configuration

Open plugins/FindTheBlock/config.yml and set your world name and disable the platform under the lobby section:

config.yml 
lobby:
  world-name: "hub"        # Exact name of your custom world folder
  platform:
    auto-generate: false   # Disable the auto-generated bedrock platform

  # Automatic void protection
  void-teleport: true
  void-teleport-blocks-above-damage: 2.0

Setup Steps

  1. Make sure your custom world folder is placed in your server directory and loaded.
  2. Open config.yml and set lobby.world-name to your world's exact name.
  3. Set lobby.platform.auto-generate: false so the 100x100 bedrock box is not built inside your world.
  4. Join the server, go to your custom lobby world, stand where you want players to spawn, and run /setworldspawn.
  5. Restart the server.

Automatic Lobby Features

When using a custom lobby world, FindTheBlock automatically manages the following protections:

  • World Spawn Integration: Players joining the lobby or returning from matches spawn at the world spawn point set by /setworldspawn.
  • Keep Inventory: keepInventory is automatically enforced on the lobby world. If a player dies, their items and XP are preserved with zero ground drops.
  • Void Fall Protection: If players slip off your lobby build into the void, they are caught 2 blocks before void damage starts and teleported back to the world spawn with fall distance reset to zero.

Starting with a Difficulty

Pass the difficulty as an argument, or choose it visually in the in-game Mode Selection GUI:

  • /start easy (Party leader ready-check)

  • /start hard

Players can check how to craft the target item at any time:

  • Run /recipe in chat.
  • A 3 × 3 crafting table GUI opens displaying the exact pattern and ingredients.
  • Works for all standard shaped and shapeless vanilla recipes.

Commands & Permissions

Command reference and permission nodes for FindTheBlock.

Admin Commands

Command Description Permission
/ftb startall [tier] Starts a match for all players currently in the lobby world. findtheblock.admin
/ftb stop / /ftb stopall Immediately cancels all active matches and returns players to lobby. findtheblock.admin
/ftb leaderboard spawn [wins|time] Spawns a floating in-world text leaderboard at your location. findtheblock.admin
/ftb leaderboard remove Removes the nearest floating leaderboard within 10 blocks. findtheblock.admin
/ftb reload Reloads the configuration file and updates floating leaderboards. findtheblock.admin

Player & Party Commands

Command Description Permission
/party, /p Opens Party Management if in a party, or Party Finder if solo. findtheblock.use
/party finder Opens the public Party Finder GUI to discover and join parties. findtheblock.use
/party create [public|private] Creates a new party (public by default). findtheblock.use
/party invite <player> Invites a player to join your party. findtheblock.use
/party accept / /party deny Accepts or declines a pending party invitation. findtheblock.use
/party leave / /party disband Leaves or disbands your current party. findtheblock.use
/party public / /party private Toggles party listing visibility in the Party Finder. findtheblock.use
/party start [tier] Starts the 20-second ready check for your party members. findtheblock.use
/start [tier] Starts a 20-second party match ready check (or admin match if OP). findtheblock.use
/join Joins an ongoing match in progress as a late racer (if enabled). findtheblock.use
/spectate, /spec Enters match world as a spectator with compass player teleportation. findtheblock.use
/leave Leaves your active match or party and returns you to the lobby. findtheblock.use
/recipe Opens crafting table recipe / guide GUI for the active target block. findtheblock.use
/menu, /selector Opens the Match Selector and Party Hub GUI (or right-click lobby compass). findtheblock.use
/top Opens the interactive leaderboard menu. findtheblock.use

Permissions Reference

  • findtheblock.admin: Grants full administrative control (default op).
  • findtheblock.play: Grants standard player gameplay commands (default all players).
  • findtheblock.bypass: Grants bypass of void lobby block protections (default op).

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about FindTheBlock.

Can players enter the Nether?

Yes. The Overworld and Nether are paired with the identical seed. Players can build and light nether portals to gather Nether items like Blaze Rods and Nether Bricks.

Can players enter the End?

Yes. The End dimension is loaded on-demand per match only if a player enters an End portal. If no one enters the End, it is not loaded. When the match ends, all dimensions (Overworld, Nether, and End) are deleted.

What happens if a player disconnects?

Players have a 60-second window to reconnect and resume where they left off. If they do not reconnect within 60 seconds, their spot is forfeited and they are treated as a late joiner.

Can players join an active match?

Yes. When a player logs in during an active round, they can use /join to join as a runner or /spectate to watch in spectator mode.

How do I reset all leaderboard stats?

Stop your server, delete plugins/FindTheBlock/stats.json, and start the server again.

Why are placeholders displaying raw text?

Make sure you have PlaceholderAPI installed on your Paper server and run /papi reload.
